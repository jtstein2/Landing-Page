I'm James. I help businesses connect AI to their everyday work, automate the processes that eat their team's time, and build custom applications that fit their operations.

Off-the-shelf software asks you to change how you work. I start the other way around: learn how your team really operates, then build the tool, integration or automation that makes the biggest difference.

Every project gets my direct attention from first conversation to handoff: clear communication, honest timelines, and no surprises on the invoice.
